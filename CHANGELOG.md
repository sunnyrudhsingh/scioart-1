# Changelog

## v1.0.0 (October 2026): first full build

The complete site structure, design and features, filled with clearly marked sample content.

### Pages

- Home: sunrise hero, "I am a…" audience chooser, this week's farm tasks, crops by season, topics, numbers, Learn & Play showcase, extension kits, advisories, how content is checked
- Crops: 36 crops across Rabi, Kharif, fruits, vegetables and allied occupations, with season filter
- Crop page with quick facts and all operations; operation guide pages with "On this page", district notes, sources and review details
- Edition archive: every operation for every Package of Practices year since 2023, with a notice on older editions
- Topics (11), each gathering guides, learning items, kits, advisories and resources
- Crop calendar from the sowing date, with phone-calendar (.ics) download
- Learn & Play: games, songs, jingles, video, e-module with quiz, farmer story, podcast, quiz
- Extension kits with seven-part template and "Download all" ZIP
- Advisories (current and archive, by district), urgent alert banner
- Resources: editions, leaflets, glossary (25 terms), research summaries
- Search across everything in both languages
- Ask an Expert form with photo upload (Supabase, or demo mode)
- Pages for farmers, extension workers, students and researchers
- About, Privacy, offline page, 404

### Platform

- Punjabi (default) and English on every page, switchable in place
- District choice (all 23 districts of Punjab), remembered on the device
- Light and dark themes
- Works offline for pages already opened (installable as an app)
- Search-engine ready: sitemap, hreflang, structured data, share images in both scripts
- Accessible: keyboard use, screen readers, large touch targets, reduced motion respected
- Content validated at build time; mistakes stop the build, never the live site
